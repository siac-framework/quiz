What's a SIAC?

SIAC stands for Self Identifying As Christian. It's an acronym to use so that we can avoid the trap of debating about which people are "real Christians" or not.

Christians don't come in a single flavor, or even a handful of flavors. Each person is different and has different elements to their faith, and how they interact with God.

If you identify as a Christian, you are probably represented somewhere in each of the categories since they represent a range of beliefs or values.

For the full descriptions and examples of each category, see the SIAC Framework here

How could I use my results?

All the topics are important and interesting, but for disarming folks in conversations who may not understand Christianity, or your particular flavor of Christianity, try explaining your values for these Categories:

    Biblical Familiarity
    Biblical Alignment
    Biblical Authority: My Body & Soul
    Biblical Authority: Other People's Bodies
    Motivational Alignment
    Political Alignment

Note that it might not be best to bring up "Evangelism" with a non-Christian unless they are already familiar with the fact that the word "evangelical" doesn't mean "weird hateful legalistic authoritarian". Feel free to point them to my full definition (linked below) so they can have a better understanding.

Why aren't the scores added into a total?

On the whole, the values in the framework are all made up, and for the most part there are no category 'scores' that are better than others. Unless you end up scoring as a racist or an authoritarian -- those are values that I can't applaud or support. They are not healthy values to have in a democratic society (or ever, really).

Scores aren't tallied because each person could have values anywhere from 1 to 5, like someone could be a 1 on church attendence if they are a solo practitioner, but could be a 5 on three topics like Biblical Familiarity/Alignment/Authority-for-self, and then a 1 on racism. Tallied scores just don't make sense. Using this as a jumping off point for conversations is what it's for.


Biblical Familiarity ↗

This topic looks to determine how familiar you are with the Bible, which is the one thing that ties all Christians together, since that's how we know anything about Jesus in the first place.
1: 'Osmosist: Never or basically never',
2: 'Osmosist: Sometimes reads the Bible',
3: 'Indirect',
4: 'Committed',
5: 'Researcher'

The closer you are to Osmosist, you get your understanding about what's in the Bible from your community. The closer you are to Researcher, the most likely you are to read the Bible for answers by yourself.

Biblical Alignment ↗

Biblical Alignment is about what parts of the Bible you align with the most (even if you don't read often).
1: 'Gospels',
2: 'Red Letter',
3: 'New Testament',
4: 'Old Testament',
5: 'Cherry Picker'

It's hard to determine in a quiz, so ymmv.

Biblical Authority: My Body & Soul ↗

This category is about how you the Bible is or isn't an authority in your life (and your life only)
1: 'Idealist',
2: 'Good Bookist',
3: 'Honorist',
4: 'Partial Literalist',
5: 'Literalist',
6: 'Literalist Separatists'

The closer you are to being an Idealist, it means you like the ideals of the Bible, but you don't think God is about rules and rule following. The closer you are to being a Literalist, it means that you think the Bible is an important authority for you in your life and you do your best to live by all the rules that you know about.

Biblical Authority: Other People's Souls ↗

This category is about whether you think the Bible is an authority for other people's Souls -- meaning whether you think the spiritual path of the Bible is true for everyone, whether or not they think the Bible is true. This is not the same category as whether the Bible is an authority for other people's behaviors or actions (their bodies) -- that's down below.
1: 'Many Paths up the Mtn',
2: 'Good People end up in Heaven',
3: 'Christ is the Only Way'

It's fine for you to beleive that Jesus is or isn't the only way, for everyone. You believing something in your heart is private and known only to you. Scoring a 3 in this category does not make you a Christian Authoritarian.

Evangelism ↗

This topic is about whether you feel that God has commanded all Christians to try and spread the gospel and convert new believers.
1: 'No one needs saving',
2: 'Not Evangelical',
3: 'Mild/Moderately Evangelical',
4: 'Life-Purpose Evangelical',
5: 'Missionary'

The closer you are to a score of 1 means that you're not evangelical, while closer to 5 means that it's the only purpose of your life on this planet. Scoring a 3, 4, or 5 does not mean you are a Christian Authoritarian. It is okay for you to follow what you believe God has commanded you to do (as long as you're being respectful of other people's boundaries if they don't want to hear the good news from you).

Motivational Alignment ↗

This topic is about what your deeper motivations are when you act on your values and beliefs. For example if you asked someone to stop doing something because it doesn't match your beliefs, or if you go so far as to try and make your beliefs and values into laws for everyone. What are the feelings or thoughts that you're having before you go the step of doing something?
1: 'None',
2: 'Live and Let Live',
3: 'Concern',
4: 'Control/Conformity',
5: 'Hate/Disgust'

This one really has to be combined with other topics in order to have meaning because you might not be someone who would say or do something to get others to change. A score of 1 or 2 just means that you wouldn't.
A score of 3 or 4 should be compared with your score on whether you would socially police everyone or just offer religious correction to Christians. Realistically, you should probably ask someone if they want advice or correction before you start, and if they don't want your advice or correction or rebuke, you should respect their boundaries and say nothing. I promise that God is big enough to get their attention and get them to change.
A score of 5 means that you need to work on some things. Jesus died for all, and that means ALL. Try reading the whole Gospel of Matthew.

Biblical Authority: Other People's Bodies ↗

This category is about how you think your religious values should be applied to other people's bodies -- whether through social policing, unsolicited correction, or even making laws that force all Americans to behave according to your religious values.
1: 'None',
2: 'Live and Let Live',
3: 'Peer-Only-Policer',
4: 'Open Season Policing',
5: 'Christian Authoritarian',
6: 'Vigilantes & Criminals'

If you scored a 1, 2, or 3, CONGRATS! You're a Pro-Democracy Christian!
If you scored a 4, you might be unknowingly leaning authoritarian -- if you're just making comments to people to try and get them to change their outward behaviors so it matches your religious values, that's not authoritarian yet, but it's on the line. Plus if you're just trying to get people to change their outward behavior, that's not going to change their hearts, and isn't the heart more important?
If you scored a 5 or 6, you are in the category of Christian Authoritarianism. That's because you are wanting to use the legal system to enforce the religious laws that you think are important, regardless of anyone else's personal or religious beliefs.

Spiritual Warfare ↗

This category is about whether you think there's a spiritual war going on,and what your role is in it.
1: 'None',
2: 'Maybe Angels',
3: 'Not My War',
4: 'Spiritual War via Prayer',
5: 'Spiritual War via Demonic Possession',
6: 'Spiritual War via Physical Violence'

A score closer to 1 means that you don't think there's a war, or if there is on it's not something that you need to play a part in. A score closer to 5 means that you def think there's a war, and you have an active role to play. A score of 5 means that, in addition to prayer, you also think that demons are active and that you or others have the ability and role to be casting them out.
A score of 1-5 does not mean you are a Christian authoritarian. However, a score of 6 means that you are reaching past the teachings of Jesus and the Epistles, who all advocated that the war is not against flesh and blood, and who all advocated for radical pacificsm. Instead of listening to them and reading their words, you are enabling or participating in violence yourself in order to speed Jesus return. If you believe God has a plan, and that it's a perfect plan, he doesn't need you to reach for violence. He's big enough to bring it about on his own.

Relationship to a Church or Parish ↗

This topic is about your relationship to a church or parish. It's not just how often you attend, but that can be part of it. Also, you don't have to go to a church.
1: 'Solo Practitioner',
2: 'Cultural',
3: 'Casual Community',
4: 'Integrated Community',
5: 'Invested/Serving/Leading'

A score closer to 1 means that you don't attend a church or that you do rarely. A score closer to 5 means that you are very integrated into your church or parish community.
This is a category that a lot of people think is the most important, but it's not. There are so many people that attend a church that isn't a best-fit for their beliefs -- whether because there isn't a church in their area that matches what they believe, or because they don't actually know what is being preached every Sunday. So if you scored anywhere from 3 to 5, make sure to check out your church's Statement of Faith, and if you donate ask to see their reports of how your donations/offerings/tithes are used.

Relationship to a Leader ↗

This category is about your relationship to a Leader. It's probably a priest or pastor, but it could be a family member, a friend, a coach, an internet personality, etc. If you look to a specific person to help guide and shape your beliefs and values, that is the leader this is about.
1: 'No Leader',
2: 'Generally respected leader',
3: 'Admired Leader, Spiritual Peer',
4: 'Adoration and Submission',
5: 'Leader over Bible',
6: 'Cult'

Scoring closer to 1 means you may not have a leader at all, or if you do, you don't put a ton of stock in their opinions or teachings. Closer to 5 means that you have a leader you respect or admire, and may follow unquestioningly even if they are leading you away from the Bible.

Political Alignment ↗

This topic is about your political alignment in a 2 party systems where the 2 parties seem to represent being "expansive" about all the things or being "regressive" about all the things. I don't believe either of these parties align with Americans or American values, but here we are.
1: 'Non-Voter',
2: 'Liberal who votes Democrat',
3: 'Conservative who votes Republican',
4: 'Liberal who votes Republican (Liberal Hostage)',
5: 'Conservative who votes Democrat'

This isn't a scale. 1, 2, and 3, are straightforward. A score of 4 is for Christians who would like to vote Liberal except for issues like abortion, so they feel stuck voting for the Conservative party. A score of 5 is for a Christian who aligns with conservative values, but feels that things are going so poorly with the conservative party that they want to vote for the other party (since we only get 2 choices in the Land of American Ingenuity).

Persecutionism ↗

This category is about whether you feel Christians are persecuted for their religious beliefs, in the US.
1: 'Christians are not persecuted',
2: 'Christians fave minor persectution',
3: 'Christians face major persecution'

Closer to 1 means that you don't think Christians are persecuted, and may even feel that they are the beneficiaries of societal Christian Priviledge. Closer to 3 means you think Christians face major religious persectution.

Racism ↗

This topic is about whether you are racist, and how that plays into your religious beliefs and practice.
1: 'All humans are equal to God',
2: 'White people are better Christians',
3: 'Christianity is only for White people'

It's pretty self explanatory. However for this category there is only 1 correct score, and that is 1.
